Commercial Ceiling Solutions

Grid, acoustic and suspended ceilings for Mumbai's offices, malls, clinics and retail — with costs and use cases.

Back to Blog

Commercial spaces have unique ceiling requirements — speed, maintenance access, acoustics and fire compliance matter as much as looks. Here's how the three main commercial systems compare, with 2026 Mumbai rates.

Grid Drop Ceilings

The most popular commercial solution: a T-grid suspension system with drop-in tiles (mineral fibre or gypsum). The killer advantage — any tile lifts out for instant access to wires, HVAC and pipes above. A 1000 sq.ft office installs in 3–5 days.

Cost: ₹150–250/sq.ft • Best for: offices, schools, banks, retail. See our grid & modular ceiling service.

Acoustic Ceilings

Specialized sound absorption for rooms where noise control matters — conference rooms, auditoriums, clinics, restaurants. Perforated gypsum boards or high-NRC mineral fibre tiles reduce echo dramatically. Add insulation above for real soundproofing.

Cost: ₹180–260/sq.ft • Best for: meeting rooms, studios, dining spaces. See our acoustic ceiling service.

Suspended (Designer) Ceilings

Gypsum board suspended ceilings give a premium, seamless look — the choice for executive offices, showrooms and hotel lobbies. Less access-friendly than grid, but far more refined visually, with integrated cove lighting options.

Cost: ₹150–250/sq.ft • Best for: reception areas, premium offices, retail flagships.

Choosing the Right System

  • Heavy overhead maintenance? Grid wins, every time.
  • Noise a problem? Acoustic, ideally with insulation.
  • Client-facing premium space? Suspended gypsum designer ceiling.
  • Fire compliance needed? Ask about fire-rated boards and access panels for any system.
