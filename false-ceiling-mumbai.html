False Ceiling — Common Questions

How much does a designer false ceiling cost in Mumbai?

Standard plain ceilings start at ₹95/sq.ft; designer multi-level cove ceilings ₹120–180/sq.ft; premium designs up to ₹250/sq.ft depending on complexity, height and lighting.

How long does installation take?

A standard room takes 2–3 days; a complete 1000+ sq.ft flat takes 7–10 days. You get a clear schedule before we start.

Is a false ceiling good for Mumbai homes?

Yes — it reduces heat by 2–4°C, improves AC efficiency, hides wiring and pipes and adds sound insulation, making it ideal for Mumbai's coastal climate.
