Your Data, Your Rights

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how R.K Ceiling Solutions ("R.K Enterprises," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal information when you visit rkceilingsolutions.com or contact us through the website. By using our website, you agree to the practices described here. This policy is aligned with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

1. Information We Collect

Information you voluntarily provide

When you fill in our quote request form or review form, we collect only what you type in:

  • Quote form: full name, phone number, email address, location (city/area), project type, approximate area in sq. ft., and project description
  • Review form: name, email address, location, star rating, and your review text
  • Any other details you share with us by phone, WhatsApp, email, or through our website chat

Information collected automatically

  • Google Analytics: pages visited, time spent on pages, referring website, browser type, device type, and approximate location (derived from your IP address). Google may collect and store additional data as described in Google's own privacy policy.
  • Usage data: IP address, operating system, and general interaction data collected by our hosting and analytics tools for security and performance purposes.

We do not collect payment card or banking details on this website, and we do not require you to create an account.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your quote request and contact you about your project
  • To arrange free site visits and provide our POP / false ceiling services
  • To publish reviews you submit (only with your permission, and only your first name and city unless you tell us otherwise)
  • To improve our website, content, and customer experience using analytics
  • To detect, prevent, and address spam, fraud, or misuse of the website
  • To comply with legal obligations

We use your information only for the purposes it was collected, and with your consent where the DPDP Act requires it. Submitting a form on our website counts as your consent for us to contact you about your enquiry.

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies (like Google Analytics cookies, e.g. _ga, _ga_*) to remember your visit and understand how the site is used. These cookies do not personally identify you — they store information such as pages visited and approximate location.

When you first visit our site, a consent banner asks permission before analytics cookies are enabled. You can accept or decline, and change your choice anytime through the "Cookie Settings" link in our footer — declining does not affect your ability to use the website. You can also control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time; most browsers let you block cookies entirely, though some website features may then work less smoothly. For more information, see Google's Cookie Policy.

4. Third-Party Services We Use

We rely on trusted service providers to run this website. They may process data on our behalf only for the purposes we describe here:

  • Google Analytics (Google LLC) — website usage analytics. Google Privacy Policy
  • EmailJS — securely delivers the details you enter in our contact/quote forms to our email inbox. EmailJS Privacy Policy
  • Tawk.to — powers the website chat / visitor notification system. tawk.to Privacy Policy
  • Elfsight — displays our Google reviews widget. Elfsight Privacy Policy
  • Google Maps — embedded map on our contact section. Google Privacy Policy
  • WhatsApp — when you tap a WhatsApp button, you leave our site and WhatsApp's terms apply. WhatsApp Privacy Policy

These providers process data under their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to read.

5. How We Share Your Information

  • We never sell or rent your personal data to anyone.
  • Form submissions are shared only with our service providers (as listed above) to deliver your enquiry to us.
  • We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect our rights and safety.
  • If the business is ever transferred or merged, your data may move with it under the same protections.

6. Data Retention

We keep your personal information only as long as it is needed for the purposes described in this policy, or as long as required by law (such as records we must keep for tax or legal purposes). When your data is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous. You can ask us to delete your enquiry details at any time (see "Your Rights" below).

7. How We Protect Your Information

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data — including HTTPS (SSL) encryption on this website and restricted access to the inboxes where enquiries arrive. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights (DPDP Act, 2023)

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Withdraw your consent at any time (this does not affect the lawfulness of processing done before withdrawal)
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact@rkceilingsolutions.com or call +91 8082005568. We will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for adults engaging us for construction and interior work. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was most recently changed. Continued use of the website after changes means you accept the updated policy.
