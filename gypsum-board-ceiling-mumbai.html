Fast, Clean & Perfectly Flat — The Gypsum Board Advantage

Gypsum board (also called plasterboard or drywall) is the modern way to build ceilings and wall linings — pre-made boards fixed on metal framing give a perfectly flat, paint-ready surface in a fraction of the time of wet plaster. R.K Ceiling Solutions provides professional gypsum board installation for ceilings, walls and partitions across Mumbai, Thane and Navi Mumbai from ₹65/sq.ft (walls).

We use only branded boards — Saint-Gobain Gyproc and USG Boral — with proper GI framing, joint tape and putty finishing. Gypsum ceilings are ideal for offices, clinics, retail and homes where speed, cleanliness and a flawless finish matter.

Gypsum Board — Common Questions

What is gypsum board used for?

False ceilings, partitions and wall linings — giving a perfectly flat, paint-ready finish much faster than wet plaster.

How much does installation cost?

Walls from ₹65/sq.ft; ceilings ₹120–180/sq.ft depending on board brand, framing and design complexity.

Are branded boards better than local ones?

Yes — branded boards (Saint-Gobain Gyproc, USG Boral) have consistent core strength and paper quality for flatter, longer-lasting results. We only use branded stock.
