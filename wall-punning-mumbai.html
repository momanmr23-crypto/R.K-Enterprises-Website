Mirror-Finish Walls, The Right Foundation for Great Paint

The best paint in the world looks bad on a bad wall. Wall punning is the cement-based putty process that creates a perfectly smooth, zero-undulation surface — so your premium paint or wallpaper shows its true colour and finish. R.K Ceiling Solutions provides professional wall punning and leveling across Mumbai, Thane and Navi Mumbai from ₹45/sq.ft.

Our process seals hairline cracks, levels uneven plaster and creates a hard, paint-ready surface that prevents peeling and patchiness for years. We handle full flats, single feature walls and pre-painting preparation for new constructions and renovations alike.

Our Wall Punning Process

1. Surface Inspection

We check plaster condition, moisture, cracks and undulation across every wall.

2. Crack & Hole Repair

Cracks widened, filled and taped; holes and chases plastered level.

3. Punning Coats

Two even coats of branded cement putty applied with straight-edges for flatness.

4. Sanding & Finishing

Fine sanding, dust wipe-down and final check — walls ready for primer and paint.

Wall Punning — Common Questions

What is wall punning and why is it needed?

Wall punning applies a cement-based putty coat for a smooth, mirror-finish surface before painting. It prevents cracks, hides imperfections and makes paint last much longer.

How much does wall punning cost in Mumbai?

Our punning starts at ₹45/sq.ft, depending on wall condition and coats required. Full flats get better package rates.

How long does it last?

Professionally done punning lasts 10–15 years without peeling, provided walls are free of active seepage — we flag any seepage during the site visit.
