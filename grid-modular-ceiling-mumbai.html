Our Commercial Installation Process

1. Site Survey & Layout Plan

We measure the space, note MEP points (HVAC, electrical, fire) and prepare a grid layout plan.

2. Itemized Commercial Quote

Per-sq.ft quote with panel brand, grid type and access panel details — transparent and fixed.

3. Perimeter & Suspension

Wall angles fixed, hangers suspended and T-grid leveled with laser precision.

4. Panel Fitting & Finishing

Tiles/boards fitted, lights and vents integrated, site cleaned daily. Handover with maintenance guidance.

Grid Ceiling — Common Questions

How fast can a grid ceiling be installed?

Grid is the fastest ceiling type — a 1000 sq.ft office is typically finished in 3–5 days, and we can work in phases or after hours to avoid disrupting your business.

Can individual tiles be replaced later?

Yes — that's the biggest advantage. Any single tile lifts out for overhead maintenance and can be replaced without touching the rest of the ceiling.

Do you work outside office hours?

Yes, for commercial clients we routinely work nights, weekends and in phases to keep your operations running.
